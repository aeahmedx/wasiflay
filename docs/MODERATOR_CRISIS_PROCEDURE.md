# Moderator crisis procedure

Internal. Not published in the app.

You are a volunteer, not an emergency service. Your job is to act fast,
document, and escalate — not to counsel anyone or investigate.

---

## Someone says they want to hurt themselves

**Do not remove the post.** Removing it isolates the person and destroys
what someone else might need to act on.

1. Reply publicly, briefly and without judgement. Something like:
   *"I'm glad you said something. Please talk to someone right now — call
   or text 988 if you're in the US or Canada. People here care."*
2. Message Ahmed immediately. Include the post link.
3. If they mention a specific plan, time, or method, that is an emergency.
   Tell Ahmed it's urgent. If you know their location and it looks
   immediate, contacting local emergency services is appropriate.
4. Remove any replies that mock, argue, or give method information.

**Never** promise confidentiality, and never say "message me privately" —
there are no private messages here, and you are not equipped for it.

---

## A threat against another person

1. Remove the content immediately.
2. Do not delete it permanently. Removal keeps it reviewable; deletion
   destroys the evidence.
3. Suspend the account.
4. Message Ahmed with the link and a description.
5. If a specific person, place, and time are named, treat it as credible.
   Ahmed decides on contacting authorities — but say so loudly and fast.

---

## Anything involving a minor

**This overrides every other rule in this document. Stop and read the
CSAM procedure before touching anything.**

Applies to: sexual content involving anyone under 18, an adult asking a
minor for photos or personal details, or an adult trying to move a
conversation with a minor somewhere private.

**Do not delete it. Do not download it. Do not share it — not even with
another moderator to ask "is this bad?"**

Remove it so nobody else sees it, suspend the account, and contact Ahmed
immediately. Say the word "urgent".

---

## Doxxing

Someone's address, workplace, immigration status, or phone number posted
without consent.

1. Remove immediately — speed matters more than certainty here.
2. Suspend if it looks deliberate rather than careless.
3. Tell Ahmed, who can delete it permanently.
4. If it's the *subject's own* information posted by themselves, leave it
   but consider telling them privately in the thread that it's public.

---

## A scam targeting the community

Money requests, fake charities, investment schemes, fake job offers.

1. Remove and suspend.
2. Tell Ahmed — the same person often comes back with a new account.
3. Post a plain warning in General if people already engaged with it.

---

## Political fights

1. Remove the thread, not just one side of it. Removing only one side is
   how a platform gets a reputation for taking sides.
2. No suspension for a first instance. Most people don't realise the rule
   exists.
3. Repeat offenders get suspended, whoever they are.

---

## What you never do

- Never act on your own content or on a dispute you're part of. Hand it
  to another moderator.
- Never discuss a specific case in public rooms.
- Never tell someone who reported them.
- Never delete permanently. That's admin-only, on purpose.
- Never promise an outcome.
